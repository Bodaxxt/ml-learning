# 📚 Week 01 - Mathematics & Foundations for Machine Learning

Welcome to Week 01. This week covers the foundational mathematics for Machine Learning, split into two main sessions:

---

## 📊 Sessions Overview

### 1. [Session 1: Statistics Fundamentals](./session-1-statistics/notes.md)
- **Why Statistics for AI?**: Data analysis, evaluation, probability.
- **Basic Definitions**: Population vs. Sample, Categorical vs. Numerical variables.
- **Descriptive Statistics**: Central tendency (Mean, Median, Mode) and Dispersion (Range, IQR, Variance, Std Dev).
- **Probability Basics**: Rules of Addition, Multiplication, and Conditional probability.
- **Bayes' Theorem**: Prior, Likelihood, Evidence, Posterior.
- **Inferential Statistics**: Central Limit Theorem (CLT), Hypothesis Testing, Confidence Intervals.

### 2. [Session 2: Linear Algebra Fundamentals](./session-2-linear-algebra/notes.md)
- **Vectors**: Ordered lists of numbers, vector operations (addition, scalar multiplication).
- **Matrices**: Data representation, linear transformations.
- **Matrix Operations**: Multiplication, Determinants, Inverses, Transposition.
- **Types of Matrices**: Square, Diagonal, Identity, Zero, Symmetric.
- **Linear Transformations**: Scaling, Rotation, Reflection, Shearing.
- **Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors**: Characteristic equation $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$.
